\section*{Phase 116 Decision-Relevant Misspecification Benchmark}

This report aggregates at the seed level. Episode-level rollouts are averaged
inside each seed before computing mean and standard deviation across seeds.
The primary claim test is whether at least one general learned method (MOPO,
RefPlan, or BA-MCTS) beats both fixed-family baselines (PLUS and MOOR-Ricker) in
a majority of decision-relevant env/action-set/seed cells under collapse-sensitive
reward. The general methods are repo-native finite-state adaptations (tabular
dynamics ensembles / categorical MLP ensemble), not the source papers' full neural
stacks.

\paragraph{Claim checks.}
Primary learned-method majority passed: \texttt{False}.
Learned win fraction (any of MOPO/RefPlan/BA-MCTS beats both baselines):
0.45 over 20 cells.
Per-method beats-both-baselines rate: MOPO 0.45; RefPlan 0.25; BA-MCTS 0.25.
RefPlan beats MOPO fraction in Allee/regime:
0.0 over 10
cells.

one seed; the claim above is based on majority behavior across cells.
