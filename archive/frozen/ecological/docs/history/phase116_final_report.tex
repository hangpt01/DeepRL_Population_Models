\section*{Phase 116 Decision-Relevant Misspecification Benchmark}

This report aggregates at the seed level. Episode-level rollouts are averaged
inside each seed before computing mean and standard deviation across seeds.
The primary claim test is whether at least one learned method (MOPO or RefPlan)
beats both fixed-family baselines (PLUS and MOOR-Ricker) in a majority of
decision-relevant env/action-set/seed cells under collapse-sensitive reward.

\paragraph{Claim checks.}
Primary learned-method majority passed: \texttt{True}.
Learned win fraction: 1.0 over
30 cells. RefPlan beats MOPO fraction in Allee/regime:
0.65 over 20
cells.

horizon 50, shared
75000-transition datasets per scenario, WandB online
logging, and frozen RefPlan/MOPO budgets recorded in the manifest. RefPlan
tuning candidates came from
$H\in\{8,10,12\}$, sequences $\in\{256,512,1024\}$, latent samples
$\in\{8,12,16\}$, $\kappa\in\{2,5,10\}$, and uncertainty penalty
$\in\{0.05,0.10,0.20\}$. MOPO tuning candidates came from horizon
$\in\{5,8\}$, rollouts $\in\{20,25,50\}$, pessimism $\lambda\in\{0.5,1,2\}$,
and epochs $\in\{50,100\}$.
