\begin{abstract}
This self-contained report extracts the accepted Phase 2E general-RL results for Amur tiger,
Crab-eating fox, and Egyptian vulture across four hidden demographic families, two observation-noise
levels, two reward modes, and four general-RL methods. All 192 requested general method-cells pass
frozen identity and per-row validity checks; no experiment was rerun. The ecological section is
safe-reward only. It reuses only ecological cells whose public-dataset hash exactly matches the
registered general-RL dataset and otherwise preserves explicit placeholders. PLUS and MOOR remain
separate methods and are not folded into a ``best ecological'' category.
\end{abstract}

\tableofcontents

\section{Experimental setting and exact comparison domain}
The exact general-RL domain is
\[
3\ \text{species}\times4\ \text{hidden families}\times2\ \sigma_{\rm obs}
\times2\ \text{reward modes}\times4\ \text{methods}=192
\]
method-cells. Hidden truth is Ricker, Allee, theta-logistic, or regime-switching;
$\sigma_{\rm obs}\in\{0.1,0.2\}$; and safe and yield rewards are reported separately.
The final ecological comparison domain is deliberately narrower: safe reward only,
$3\times4\times2=24$ cells for each ecological method.

\subsection{Methods}
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[RefPlan-inspired.] A reference-policy planner with a separately fitted public policy prior,
re-evaluated at every simulated continuation state; the policy prior and model posterior remain
distinct fitted objects.
\item[OGSRL-inspired.] An offline actor with a normalized 25-step discounted low-abundance safety
cost, pathwise model/process uncertainty, a dual constraint, an OOD/safety guardian, and a
least-violating fallback. Reported outcomes belong to this deployed composite, not to the learned
actor alone.
\item[BA-MCTS-inspired.] Bootstrap-ensemble epistemic tree search using the frozen depth-eight,
256-simulation configuration.
\item[EVD/value-disagreement pessimism.] Bootstrap Q-ensemble value-disagreement pessimism,
Delphic-motivated. It is not a fitted latent-compatible-world Delphic method and is not described
as measuring Delphic uncertainty.
\end{description}

\subsection{Why these species}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Amur tiger---declining but recoverable:} a recovery problem in which interventions
can reverse decline.
\item \textbf{Crab-eating fox---strong-growth/recoverable:} a contrasting high-growth recoverable
case, useful for detecting unnecessary or overly conservative intervention.
\item \textbf{Egyptian vulture---sink:} a demographic sink whose safe reward is strongly negative
and whose zero collapse-entry rate can coexist with continuous unsafe occupancy.
\end{itemize}
This selection follows ecological Option A and intentionally supports separate recoverable and sink
interpretation; it is not representative of all nine registered populations.

\section{Data, evaluation, and aggregation}
Each public dataset contains exactly 4,000 logged transitions from 160 complete 25-step episodes.
The split is episode-preserving 80/20: 128 complete training episodes and 32 complete holdout
episodes, so transitions from an episode never cross the split. The same public dataset and
collection seed are shared by all four general methods within a species--family--noise--reward cell.
The frozen dataset registry preserves ecological collection seeds and action channels.

Each method-cell has 20 evaluation episodes: five registered seed blocks
\(\{7001,7051,7101,7151,7201\}\), four episodes per seed, and 50 steps per episode.
Operational return is the signed 50-step discounted return with \(\gamma=0.95\). Table means and SDs
are the mean and sample dispersion across the 20 evaluation episodes within a cell. Compact
summaries first reduce each cell to its 20-episode mean and then average the eight family--noise
cells. These 20 episodes share one fitted policy and are evaluation replicates, not 20 independent
fits. Collapse entry means entering the safety region from above; unsafe fraction and MVP breach
must be read with it. ``Min.\ \(N\)'' is mean episode minimum abundance. Fallback is mean recorded
fallback decisions per episode.

\section{Safety, fallback, and constraint interpretation}
The full tables retain collapse entry, unsafe fraction, MVP breach, persistence, economic cost,
and fallback counts. For a sink already below its safety threshold, collapse entry can remain zero
while unsafe fraction and MVP breach remain one and persistence remains zero. It must never be used
alone as a safety claim.

The evaluator records 27,204 OGSRL fallback events over 144,000 decisions in the full nine-species
experiment (18.89\%). The frozen counter combines (i) exceptions from policy action selection,
(ii) invalid action IDs, and (iii) the intentional \texttt{hard\_fallback} diagnostic used when the
guardian finds no feasible action. Consequently, OGSRL fallback counts may combine multiple causes.
The results are scientifically usable as outcomes of the deployed actor + guardian +
least-violating-fallback composite, but not as fallback-free actor-only outcomes. RefPlan, BA-MCTS,
and EVD have zero recorded evaluator fallback in the frozen experiment. The validity receipt gates
identity, fit/mechanism, resources, threads, and successful exit; it does not reject a row merely
because the OGSRL composite invoked its fallback.

\section{Limitations and reporting boundaries}
\begin{enumerate}
\item This is a deliberately selected three-species benchmark, not a nine-population result.
\item Four thousand transitions (160 logging episodes) may be inadequate for rare states, rare
actions, and family-specific dynamics. Episode-level holdout prevents leakage but does not guarantee
support in dangerous regions.
\item The four general Crab-eating-fox/theta datasets (safe/yield at both noise levels) use the exact
registered 160-complete-episode subset because the ecological source contained five partial rows.
They satisfy the frozen general design but are not whole-file identical to that source. This is why
the two legacy safe PLUS theta cells are not inserted as exact matched values.
\item Within-cell SD describes evaluation-episode dispersion conditional on one fitted policy; it is
not uncertainty over independent datasets or fits.
\item OGSRL results describe the deployed composite and its fallback counter is cause-conflated.
\item EVD is Delphic-motivated value-disagreement pessimism, not a full Delphic compatible-world
construction.
\item Corrected MOOR is available only for Amur tiger and Egyptian vulture and is behaviorally
non-discriminating against PLUS on that overlap.
\item The frozen 576-row outputs store episode summaries, not per-timestep trajectories or episode
maximum abundance. A separate validated diagnostic supplies trajectories for Amur tiger,
Puerto Rican parrot, and Egyptian vulture, not Crab-eating fox, so it is not substituted into this
exact Option A three-species report.
\end{enumerate}
