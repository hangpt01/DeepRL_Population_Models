## 4. Implementation results

### Information boundary

- The exact registered 13-key order, shapes, dtypes and classifications are encoded.
- All ten forbidden private fields are rejected by registered name.
- `states` and `next_states` have separate future capabilities; neither is authorized in
  I2A, and `next_states` is structurally rejected at runtime/deployment/action-selection/
  planning-root interfaces.
- The metadata-only NPZ inspector reads headers for all keys and payload only for
  `metadata_json`, returning only `public_dataset_sha256`.
- Instrumentation reports zero scientific payload fields, zero scientific payload bytes,
  and zero real scientific-array materializations.
- Missing, extra, reordered, wrong-dtype, wrong-shape, wrong-unit, nonfinite,
  row-misaligned and incorrectly bound synthetic inputs all fail closed.

### Exact-state and preprocessing contracts

- Float64 column 1 is assigned literal positive `0.0`; no weighted-moment result or
  equality detector constructs the primary value.
- Columns 2–4 are bit-exact copies of column 0; the feature count/order is fixed.
- Columns 6–9, action history and observation history are compared before/after through
  independently captured snapshots and distinct hashes. Mutations to protected
  features 7–9, either history, shape, dtype or ordering fail.
- Sigma and observation scale must exceed the registered `1e-12` zero-likelihood
  boundary; zero/epsilon routes fail.
- The inclusive `std <= 1e-8` guard, explicit mask/offset/scale, literal scale `1.0`,
  literal-zero transform, `1e-6` runtime tolerance, alias map, rank-one state block and
  offline/runtime artifact parity are implemented across canonical strict-JSON
  serialization and reload into a distinct artifact object.
- The executable receipt records Arm O rank five versus Arm T rank one, mask columns
  1/5/9, exact aliases, zero maximum SD, artifacts, RefPlan offsets and RNG parity.

### Method and interpretation contracts

- PLUS/MOOR require raw abundance divided by `survey_scale`, a nearest latent bin, an
  explicit registered raw-grid round trip and a one-hot point mass. They do not use the
  public zero-SD preprocessing branch.
- RefPlan has distinct primary end-to-end and optional frozen-fit secondary contracts;
  the latter must be explicitly enabled and requires complete valid, byte/hash-identical
  Arm O/T preprocessing and all learned artifacts.
- OGSRL's dataset-derived safety budget/calibration is an explicit changed axis.
- RefPlan/BA-MCTS dynamics, `residual_sigma` and model-posterior sharpness are explicit
  possible changed axes.
- EVD's unmatched raw-reward objective requires separate future reporting.
- Any changed preprocessor, feature fit, dynamics, residual sigma, surrogate, reward,
  safety calibration, ensemble, policy or other learned artifact forces
  `MODEL-FIT AXIS CHANGED — END-TO-END BUNDLE ONLY` and bars frozen-fit/causal labels.
- For RefPlan, PLUS and MOOR, every one of the eight registered SHA identities must be
  exactly 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters and `residual_sigma_hex` must be a
  nonempty tuple of canonical finite float64 hex strings in both arms. Missing,
  placeholder, sentinel, malformed, prefixed, uppercase, or unresolved identities fail
  closed before equality is evaluated.
- Mandatory RefPlan values are finite numeric strict JSON; registered tiger `-2.7018`
  and fox `-2.7348` offsets and mismatch-removal semantics are enforced.
